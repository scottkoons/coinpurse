Privacy Policy

Coin Purse, by Yetignome. Last updated October 18, 2026.

Coin Purse is made by Yetignome ("we"). It is an iPhone app that keeps your passes, QR codes and cards in one private place on your iPhone. There is no account and no sign in. This policy explains what happens to the things you add.

What we collect

Nothing. Coin Purse has no servers that hold your data, and we never receive your coins, pictures, notes, pins or anything else you add. We do not use analytics, tracking tools or ads, and we do not sell or share any data.

Where your coins live

Every coin and picture is kept only on your iPhone, in the app's own storage. Coin Purse works completely with no internet: making coins, pictures, voice notes, Face ID hiding, the archive and search all happen on your iPhone. Your coins do not sync between devices.

  • Pictures. When you paste or pick a picture, only that one picture is saved in Coin Purse. We do not read your photo library or your contacts.
  • Voice notes. Voice notes are turned into text by Apple speech recognition, which runs on your iPhone when your iPhone supports it; otherwise Apple may process it under Apple's own privacy policy and your iPhone's settings. Coin Purse never saves the recording; only the text you choose to save is kept, on your iPhone.
  • Map pins. Your location is read only when you tap Pin where I am now, Add Pin or Move Pin Here: Coin Purse reads it once and saves that spot on the coin, on your iPhone, so you can get directions back to it. It never tracks your location in the background, and the spot is deleted with the coin. To draw a map of a pin, your iPhone gets the map from Apple Maps, as any map app does, and directions open in Apple Maps.

Sharing

Nothing leaves your iPhone unless you share it. When you share a picture or a pin from Coin Purse, you choose the app and the person in the iOS share sheet, and only that picture, or that spot as an Apple Maps link, is shared. When you share something into Coin Purse from another app, it is saved straight onto your iPhone.

Backups and deleting

Your coins are included in your iPhone's normal iCloud Backup, which Apple keeps under your Apple Account and Apple's privacy policy. Restoring a new iPhone from that backup brings them back. Deleting a coin removes it and its pictures (after the few seconds when Undo is offered). To delete everything, delete the Coin Purse app; its coins and pictures are deleted with it.

Moving from the earlier version

An earlier test version of Coin Purse kept coins on a Coin Purse server, hosted by Vercel in the United States. Those coins were copied onto their owners' iPhones, and the server and everything on it have been deleted. Coin Purse no longer has a server that holds any of your data.

Security

You can lock the app with Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode, and hide any coin with Face ID. Your coins are protected by your iPhone's own security. Hiding is not encryption, so keep credit cards, IDs and important passwords in Apple Wallet or Passwords.

Children

Coin Purse is not directed to children under 13. We do not collect data from anyone, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
