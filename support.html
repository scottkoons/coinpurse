Coin Purse Support

A simple app for simple things. Help for the Coin Purse iPhone app.

Coin Purse is a free iPhone app for the small, temporary things you need later: tickets, gift cards, parking spots, badges, codes and quick notes. Your coins are saved to your private account, so they come back when you sign in on a new iPhone. Please do not store credit cards, IDs or passwords in it.

What to keep in it

Anything you want to pull up quickly that does not need to be locked away: conference badges, tailgate and event tickets (several in one coin), the barcode on a loyalty or haircut card, gift card codes, return labels, a link someone texted you, someone's email address, where you parked, a sign you want to remember, a handwritten list. Keep it as long as you like, or toss it when you are done.

Signing in

Enter your email address and we send you a 6-digit code. Type the code into Coin Purse and you are in. You stay signed in, and there is no password to remember. If the code does not arrive within a minute, check your spam or junk folder, then request a new one. Codes expire after 15 minutes.

Your purse

Your coins sit in a stack of colored cards, like Apple Wallet. Tap a card to open it, and it slides out of the stack. While a coin is open, swipe sideways to flip to the next or previous coin, or tap one of the coins peeking up at the bottom. To close it, tap Done or drag the card down.

In a bigger purse, tap the magnifying glass to search titles and notes. To change the order, touch and hold a card and choose Rearrange.

Adding a coin

The glass bar at the bottom of your purse has three buttons: Picture, Voice and Pin.

  • Picture. Tap Paste to add a screenshot or picture you copied (one tap, no prompt), choose from Photos, or snap one with the Camera. Then tap Save. A coin holds up to six pictures, and each one shows as a thumbnail under the open card. Tap a thumbnail to see it full size.
  • Voice. Say a quick note, like where you parked or who to call back. Your words appear as you talk. Tap the stop button, check the text, and tap Save.
  • Pin. Saves where you are right now on a map.

A title is optional. Untitled coins are named Coin 1, Coin 2 and so on. To add pictures or change the title, notes or color later, open the coin and tap Edit.

Voice notes

Apple speech recognition on your iPhone turns your voice into text. It is free, and the recording is never saved; only the text you choose to save is kept. A voice note becomes a coin you can add a picture to later with Edit. If Coin Purse did not catch what you said, tap Keep talking to try again.

Pins and walking directions

Tap Pin to save your spot. The open card shows a live Apple map with your pin and a ring that shows how accurate the spot is. Tap the map for walking directions in Apple Maps. Maps shows the route and waits for you to tap Go. To change a pin later, open the coin, tap Move Pin, and choose Move Pin Here or Remove Pin.

Coin Purse asks for your location only when you tap Pin. It never tracks you in the background. If Location is off for Coin Purse, open Settings, then Apps, then Coin Purse, then Location, and choose While Using the App. Turn on Precise Location there too, or your pin could be off by a long way. If Precise Location is off, Coin Purse asks to use it once, just for that pin.

Showing a QR code or barcode

Open a coin and tap a picture to see it full size. When the picture has a QR code or barcode, the screen brightness turns all the way up so scanners can read it, and goes back to normal when you leave. Swipe to see the other pictures in the coin.

Tapping links and copying text

Web addresses, email addresses and phone numbers in your pictures and notes are tappable. Press and hold any text in a picture, like a gift card code, to select and copy it. This uses Apple's Live Text and happens on your iPhone.

Sharing into Coin Purse from other apps

In Photos, Messages, Safari, Mail or almost any other app, tap Share and choose Coin Purse. Pick New Coin to make a coin from it, or Add to a Coin to add it to a coin you already have. If Coin Purse is not in the list, scroll to the end of the app row, tap More, and turn it on. You need to be signed in to Coin Purse on that iPhone first.

Sharing a picture from Coin Purse

Open a coin and tap Share to send its pictures, or, for a pin, a link to the spot in Apple Maps. To send just one picture, tap it to see it full size, then tap Share. Only that one picture is sent.

Home Screen quick actions

Touch and hold the Coin Purse icon on your Home Screen and choose Pin My Spot, Voice Note or Add Picture to jump straight in.

Siri, Shortcuts and the Action Button

Say “Pin my spot in Coin Purse,” “New voice note in Coin Purse,” “Add a picture to Coin Purse” or “Show a coin in Coin Purse.” The same actions are in the Shortcuts app. On an iPhone with an Action Button, open Settings, then Action Button, choose Shortcut, and pick a Coin Purse action.

iPhone Search

Swipe down on your Home Screen and type a coin's title to find it and open it. Only titles show up in iPhone Search. Your notes never do.

Lock with Face ID

Tap the account button at the top of your purse and turn on Lock with Face ID. Coin Purse then asks for Face ID when you open it after a minute away.

Using it offline

Your saved purse and pictures open with no signal, like in a parking garage or a packed stadium. A small Offline label shows while you are not connected.

Deleting a coin

Tap the trash can on a card, or open a coin and tap Delete. Coin Purse asks first, then deletes the coin and all of its pictures. This cannot be undone.

Light mode, dark mode and text size

Coin Purse follows your iPhone's light or dark setting. It supports larger text sizes and VoiceOver.

Signing out and lost phones

Tap the account button at the top of your purse, then Sign out. If you lose a phone, sign in on another iPhone, open Account and choose Sign out of all devices. Every other phone will need a new email code.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Account, then Delete Account. Your account, every coin and every picture are permanently deleted right away, on all devices.
