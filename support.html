Coin Purse Support

A simple app for simple things. Help for the Coin Purse iPhone app.

Coin Purse is a private wallet for the pictures you need to show: conference QR codes, loyalty cards, tickets and passes. Your coins are saved to your account, so they come back when you sign in on a new iPhone. Please do not store credit cards, IDs or passwords in it.

What to keep in it

Anything you want to pull up quickly that does not need to be locked away: conference badges, tailgate and event tickets (several in one coin), the barcode on a loyalty or haircut card, gift card codes, return labels, a link someone texted you, someone's email address, the row you parked in, a sign you want to remember, a handwritten list. Keep it as long as you like, or delete it when you are done. Please do not store credit cards, IDs or passwords.

Tapping links and copying text

Open a coin and tap a web address, email address or phone number in the picture to open it. Press and hold any text, like a gift card code, to select and copy it. This uses Apple's Live Text and happens on your iPhone. Links in a coin's notes are tappable too.

Signing in

Enter your email address and we send you a 6-digit code. Type the code into Coin Purse and you are in. You stay signed in, and Face ID unlocks the app. There is no password to remember. If the code does not arrive within a minute, check your spam or junk folder, then request a new one. Codes expire after 15 minutes.

Adding a coin

Tap +, then Paste a screenshot you copied, choose from Photos, or snap one with the Camera, and tap Save. A title is optional: untitled coins are named Coin 1, Coin 2 and so on. Each coin holds a main picture and up to 5 more.

Sharing a picture

Open a coin, swipe to the picture you want, and tap Share. Only that one picture is sent.

Lost phone

Sign in on another iPhone, open Account and choose Sign out of all devices. Every other phone will need a new email code.

Deleting your account

In the app, open Account, then Delete Account. Your account, coins and pictures are permanently deleted right away.
