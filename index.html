Coin Purse

For the quick stuff: QR codes, passes and cards.

Coming soon to the iPhone App Store

What it is

Apple Wallet is for the valuable things. Coin Purse is for the small change: anything you want to pull up fast that does not need to be locked away or kept forever. Snap a photo or paste a screenshot, and it is a coin, ready when you need it.

Good for

  • Conference badge and event check-in QR codes.
  • Return labels and return QR codes.
  • Any QR code you want to find again without digging.
  • A photo of a handwritten note, shopping list or to-do list.
  • The useful part of an email, like directions or simple instructions.
  • Punch cards and loyalty cards.

How it works

  • One tap to Paste a screenshot, or snap a photo.
  • No typing needed: untitled coins are named Coin 1, Coin 2 and so on.
  • Swipe through your coins like cards in a wallet.
  • Locked with Face ID and private to your account.
  • Share a single picture when someone needs it.

Coin Purse is for everyday things. Please do not store credit cards, IDs or passwords in it.
