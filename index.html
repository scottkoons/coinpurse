What it is

Apple Wallet is for the important things. Coin Purse is for everything else: the conference badge QR code, the haircut punch card, the parking pass, the gym check-in. Paste a screenshot with one tap and it is ready whenever you need to show it.

  • One-tap Paste of any screenshot or picture.
  • Swipe through your coins like cards in a wallet.
  • Locked with Face ID, private to your account.
  • Share a single picture when someone needs it.

Coin Purse is for everyday passes and QR codes. Please do not store credit cards, IDs or passwords in it.
