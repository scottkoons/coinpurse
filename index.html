What is in a coin?

One coin, everything that goes together.

A coin is one thing you want handy, and it can hold up to six pictures. Two tailgate tickets are one coin, not two. Same for the front and back of a card, or every page of a return label.

  1. 1Find it. The badge on the card shows how many pictures the coin holds.
  2. 2Tap to open. The coin fills the screen, ready to scan.
  3. 3Swipe for the next one. Ticket 2 is right there. Pinch to zoom on any picture.
  4. 4Tap outside to close. Back to your purse.

Tap what you see

Links, emails and numbers just work.

Coin Purse reads the text in your pictures right on your iPhone. Tap a web address to open it, tap an email address to write to it, tap a phone number to call. Press and hold any text, like a gift card code, to copy it.
