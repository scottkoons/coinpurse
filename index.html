Life hands you small change.

The level you parked on. The tailgate tickets. The gift card in your drawer. A link a friend texted you. It all ends up buried in thousands of photos, or lost somewhere in your email.

2

Snap it.

Take a photo right into Coin Purse: the garage sign, the back of a card, a note someone handed you. It never clutters your photo library.

3

Or paste it.

Screenshot a ticket, an email or a web page, then tap Paste once. No "Allow Paste" prompt and no typing. Prefer an older photo? Pick it from your library.

4

Now it is a coin.

It lands at the front of your purse. Skip the title and it is named Coin 5, Coin 6 and so on. Add a name, a note or a color only when you want to.

5

Flip through your purse.

Swipe the stack and the front coin goes to the back, like cards in a wallet. Everything you saved is a flick away.

6

One coin, both tickets.

A coin holds up to six pictures. Tap to open it full screen, ready to scan, and swipe to the next one. Two tailgate tickets, one coin.

7

Tap what you see.

Coin Purse reads the text in your pictures right on your iPhone. Tap a link to open it, an email address to write to it, a phone number to call. Press and hold to copy a gift card code.

8

Toss it when you are done.

Every coin has a trash can right on the card. Back from the trip? One tap and the parking photo is gone, without ever touching your camera roll. Or keep a coin as long as you like.

9

Yours alone.

Face ID unlocks the app. Sign in once and stay signed in. Your coins are private to your account, with no ads and no tracking.

What is in a coin?

One coin, everything that goes together.

A coin is one thing you want handy. It can hold up to six pictures, so two tailgate tickets are one coin, not two. Same for the front and back of a card, or every page of a return.

🏷️

A name, if you want one

Type one, or skip it and the coin is named Coin 1, Coin 2 and so on.

🖼️

Up to six pictures

Photos, screenshots or both. The first is the one you see on the card.

📝

Notes

A gift card code, a seat number, a link. Links, emails and phone numbers are tappable.

🎨

A color

A stripe across the top so you can spot it in the stack.

What people keep in it

Small change, everywhere.

Conference badges

Scan in at the door without digging through your email.

Tailgate and event tickets

Two QR codes, one coin. Swipe between them at the gate.

Gift cards

Snap the back of the card. At checkout they scan the barcode, or you copy the number.

A number to call

Add a note like the airport shuttle number, then tap it to call.

Return labels

Keep the return QR code ready for the drop-off counter.

Where you parked

Snap the level sign in the airport garage. Delete it when you are home.

Links people text you

Screenshot it now, tap the link later.

Someone's email address

Grab it, send your email with one tap, then toss the coin.

Grocery lists

The handwritten list on the counter. Snap it and go shopping.
