Every option

Everything it does.

A small app with a lot of handy shortcuts. Here is all of it.

  • A purse like Wallet

    Your coins sit in a stack of colored cards. Tap one to open it, and tap Done or drag it down to put it back. Pull down and the cards fan apart.

  • Picture

    Tap New Coin, then Picture. Paste in one tap with no prompt, pick from Photos, or use the Camera. A coin holds up to six pictures.

  • Voice notes

    Tap New Coin, then Voice Note, and say it. It is saved as text for free, and the audio is never saved.

  • Typed notes

    Tap New Coin, then Typed Note, for a code, a list or a reminder. A picture is optional.

  • Pin your spot

    Tap Pin where I am now in a new coin, or Add Pin on an open one, to save where you are on a live map. Tap the map for walking directions in Apple Maps.

  • Bright for scanners

    Open a picture full size, and if it has a QR code or barcode the screen turns all the way up. It goes back when you leave.

  • Tappable links

    Links, email addresses and phone numbers in your notes open with a tap. In a picture, press and hold any text to copy it.

  • Share from any app

    Share from Photos, Messages, Safari or Mail. Make a new coin, or add to a coin you already have.

  • Quick actions

    Touch and hold the app icon for Pin My Spot, Voice Note or Add Picture.

  • Siri, Shortcuts and the Action Button

    Ask Siri to pin your spot, start a voice note, add a picture or show a coin, like “Pin my spot in Coin Purse.” The same actions work in Shortcuts and on the Action Button.

  • iPhone Search

    Type a coin’s title in iPhone Search to open it. Only titles show up there, never your notes.

  • Peek, move and search

    Touch and hold a card to lift it and see what is on it, then drag it to put your coins in any order, or tap Rearrange for a list. Search a big purse by title or note.

  • Swipe to delete, with Undo

    Swipe a coin to the left, like Mail, or tap its trash can. Every delete shows Undo for a few seconds.

  • Face ID lock

    Turn on Lock with Face ID, and it asks again after a minute away. You sign in with a code sent to your email.

  • Works offline

    Your saved purse and pictures open with no signal, deep in a parking garage or a packed stadium.

  • Light and dark

    Coin Purse follows your iPhone, in light mode or dark mode.

  • Larger text and VoiceOver

    Supports larger text sizes and VoiceOver, so everyone can find their things.

  • Free and private

    Free, with no ads, in your own private account. Please do not store credit cards, IDs or passwords in it.
